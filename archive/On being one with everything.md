## Exploration

When I breathe in, and the air becomes me Therefore I am air

When I drink water, and water becomes me Therefore I am water

When I eat the grass of the earth, the grass becomes me Therefore I am the grass

When I eat an animal, the animal becomes me Therefore I am all the animals

When I die, I will become the earth Therefore the earth is me
