## 7.1 Design Philosophy
### Home Server Mission Statement
This server is designed as a private, self-hosted cloud to replace commercial services like Google Drive and Google Photos. Its primary goals are data sovereignty, centralised management, automation, and providing secure, reliable access to data and services for family and friends. The system prioritises stability, ease of maintenance, and clear documentation to ensure long-term resilience.
