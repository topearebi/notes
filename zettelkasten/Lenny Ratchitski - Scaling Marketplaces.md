## Insights

> A marketplace business hels demand find supply and leads to a financial transaction
> 

Marketplaces need a financial element to be considered markets. Having a demand side and a supply side does not necessarily form a market. You must enable finance, and you must control it

> Nothing matters if you don’t have product market fit
> 

Product market fit is the only way to grow. Because you have solved a problem, provided an advantage. Accomplished a job, removed a pain point, etc. You have scaled the hurdle of willingness. The rest is solving for efficiency.

> Supply is king. Supply growth unlocks demand.
> 

If you have no supply to fulfil the demand, you have no market. You can get demand, but as long as there is no supply to fulfil it, they will leave. With supply, however, users will come because they know they can get what they want. Supply is also business. They can hold space in multiple markets and operate. Demand only needs one market and will “attrit” if its needs are not met.

> You may think you’re a marketplace, but you might not be…Is there a demand for the supply you’re offering?
> 

Some businesses may start out offering a market for a service. However, the fact that a service exists, does not mean there is a need for it to be “productised”. There may be no added value in the aggregation. A business may also not meet the marketplace definition, if b) it does not have the financial component/transaction element.

> Median number of supply-growth levers is 2
> 

There are many strategies to grow your supply base. Or supply of supply. However, businesses on average are only capable of leveraging two of these levers. Anymore could overstretch resources or lead to diminishing returns [pareto’s principles](https://www.notion.so/pareto-s-principles-8f3d473b3f2544cb99e3f21b32e0a9fb?pvs=21) 

> At scale, there are no silver bullets to accelerate your growth forever. Its a lot of lead bullets..continue to add more growth levers and layer them on
> 

When growing a marketplace business, there is no perfect strategy. While in the initial stage, 2 levers are pulled so to speak, when scaling at the growth stage, you continue to pull levers to see growth.

## Summary

The talk is in 2 parts and is an analysis is multiple marketplace businesses

### The chicken and egg problem

There are 4 steps to solving this

**Constrain**

Limit your viable operating space.

You can constrain by geography, or by category.

Alternatively, constraining may mean your TAM is insignificant and so you forego constraining

**Concentrate**

Concentrate on one side of the marketplace

Most marketplaces focused on growing their supply capabilities because supply unlocks demand. 

Some unique marketplaces however have pretty fluid and flexible supply bases that can be easily onboarded. eg flexible work

Some supply is also dependent on existing data and so can be easily sourced at the start allowing you to focus on growing demand

**Supply**

Grow your initial supply

12 common growth supply levers

1-3: Direct sales, piggyback (off an existing network), referrals

4-12: word of mouth, subsidize (Uber), employees as early supply (doordash), single player mode (focus on supply without a promise of demand), performance marketing (surprise), loops, events, SEO (surprise), Community

Most companies only used 2 of these levers

**Demand**

Grow your initial demand

1-3: Word of mouth (PMF), Supply drove demand, SEO

4-12: Performance marketing, PR, Loops, Referrals, Direct sales, Events, Single-player mode, Partnerships, Mobile

Product market fit was essential which is why 

### The scaling problem

How do we scale this thing that’s working?

The four most common questions from growing marketplaces

**How to determine if you are supply or demand-constrained, or does it depend on the market/category?**

Your constraint determines your ability to grow. Do you need more service providers to increase your transactions, or more demand to onboard your suppliers?

**What growth levers work at scale?**

There are fewer levers at scale but companies employ on average 3

1-8: Performance marketing, geo expansion, conversion, SEO, Direct sales, Referrals, Loops, PR

**How to maintain quality?**

Standards+penalties, Manually onboard, Review, Search ranking, Differentiator, Customer service, Early signals (qualify, test), Add friction, Set an example

**What would you do differently if you did it again?**

1. More focus
2. More more aggressive
3. Think longer term
4. Focus on PMF before scaling
5. Leverage data sooner
6. Build more empathy for supply
7. Expand into new business earlier
