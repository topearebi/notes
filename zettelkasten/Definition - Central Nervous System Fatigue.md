This is a decline in the efficiency and functioning of the CNS usually due to high intensity activities requiring maximal effort like weightlifting or sprinting. CNS fatigue often results in reduced brain-muscle neural drive, decreased strength, power, coordination and reaction time. The changes are also neurochemical and can alter one’s perception of motivation, perception of effort and motor control due to its effects on serotonin, dopamine and noradrenaline.
