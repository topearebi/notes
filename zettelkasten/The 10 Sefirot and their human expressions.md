The 10 Sefirot are described as divine channels of attribute and energy. Here are their names and primary functions as explained in the video (0:52-3:02):

1. Chochmah (0:52): The initial seed of an idea; inspiration.
2. Binah (1:05): The development, logic, and organization of ideas.
3. Da'at (1:15): The focus that makes an idea relevant and personally meaningful.
4. Chesed (1:29): The power to love, share, and look kindly upon others.
5. Gevurah (1:40): The power of discipline, restraint, and calculation.
6. Tiferet (1:50): The power to empathize and harmonize kindness with strictness.
7. Netzach (2:06): The power of determination and persistence to overcome challenges.
8. Hod (2:17): The power of humility, gratitude, and submission.
9. Yesod (2:29): The power to focus on others and build genuine relationships.
10. Malchut (2:42): The power of speech, action, leadership, and receptivity.
