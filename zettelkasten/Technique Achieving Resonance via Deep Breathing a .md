# Technique: Achieving Resonance via Deep Breathing and Projection

Vocal resonance can be enhanced through specific breathing techniques. This involves taking infrequent but sufficient breaths that feel like they reach the bottom of the lungs, causing slight abdominal expansion on inhalation. Breath should be drawn slowly and released gently during speech to extend vocalizations and project the voice effectively beyond the speaker's immediate personal space.
