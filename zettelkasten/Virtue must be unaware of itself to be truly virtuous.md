True, or superior, virtue is characterized by a lack of intention or self-awareness of being virtuous. When virtuous actions arise naturally from one's character without the explicit goal *of being seen as* or *feeling* virtuous, they embody genuine virtue. Conversely, the moment virtue becomes a conscious goal or performance, it risks losing its authenticity, aligning with Lao Tzu's concept of "inferior virtue.
