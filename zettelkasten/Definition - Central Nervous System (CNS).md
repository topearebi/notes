The CNS is a system of the body consisting of the brain and spinal cord. Its function is to receive sensory information, process it and send out instructions to the rest of the body as motor signals. The system thus controls movement, thought, sensation, 

The CNS is differentiated from the [peripheral nervous system](peripheral%20nervous%20system%201f8635c581e080d4b170da1f21dd2a4b.md)
