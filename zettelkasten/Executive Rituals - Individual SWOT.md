For board meetings, it is important to establish rituals for executive members in order to drive strategy conversations. Rituals are determined by what is important to an organisation but a good one is to examine what is top of mind for each board member

A good framework for that is the SWOT analysis so each board member informs the board of weaknesses, strengths, opportunities and threats that have been top of mind for them and that helps keep the board focused on business improvement.
