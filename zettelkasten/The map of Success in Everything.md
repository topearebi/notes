# The map of Success in Everything

You can either take money, or be given money

You can take it by force, or be given it in exchange for a product or service

If you wish to exchange for a product or service, you can either charge once, or recurringly

For people to buy your product or service, they need to 1. be aware. 2. trust

Awareness → Media channels, people, advertising, referrals

Trust → Testimonials, previous use(returning customer, Team(Owner)

Increase trust
