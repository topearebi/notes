In the contemporary landscape dominated by new media, the definition and perception of "overwhelming evidence" is complex. Key questions arise: Does evidence derive its power from its inherent credibility and truthfulness, or from its ability to *confer* credibility or truth upon the object or claim it supports? How do characteristics of modern media, such as speed, volume, and potential for manipulation, impact what is considered convincing evidence by the public or experts?
