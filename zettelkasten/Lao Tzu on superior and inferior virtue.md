Lao Tzu presents a distinction between two levels of virtue based on self-awareness:

> "The man of superior virtue is not conscious of his virtue, and in this way he really possesses virtue. The man of inferior virtue never loses sight of his virtue, and in this way he loses his virtue."
> 

Lao Tzu - Tao Te Ching

This suggests that true virtue operates without self-consciousness, while virtue that is constantly aware of itself is somehow lesser or perhaps performative.
