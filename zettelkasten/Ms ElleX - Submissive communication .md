### Background

Hand signals are a training modality to explore

- They can speed up communication

Hand signals exist in different forms or languages already. Military, ASL, etc and you can modify or invent new ones to train in.

### Considerations

You may want to ensure that your signals can be done with one hand to ensure that you can do them in any situation
