# Stock-Trading Terms

### Stock splitting

Stock splitting: The act of splitting one stock into multiple parts. This also splits the price into an equal number of parts. It however increases the number of shares available and trading volume which can help artificially drive prices up.

### Support and resistance

Resistance is the price point or zone at which a stock’s price is higher that the market’s desire to buy.

Support is the price point or price zone at which the market is more willing to buy a stock than to sell it.

One strategy for making money off the stock market through day trading is to identify the support and resistance zones. You can buy at the support zone for the day and sell when it is close to resistance
