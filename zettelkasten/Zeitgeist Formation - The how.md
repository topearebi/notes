The prevailing spirit or mood of a particular time, known as the zeitgeist, appears to form not through isolated individual actions but rather through a collective, concerted movement or confluence of many individuals and influences. This suggests that cultural shifts are emergent properties of widespread interaction and shared experience, rather than the result of single efforts.

Source: Reflection on "Of Course We’re Living in a Simulation," Jason Kehe, Wired
