## Ku Naming
You need to familiarise yourself with your ku (subconscious)
The first step in this process is to name your ku so that it is easy to refer to it. Give it an identity so that you start to understand it as it's own system with knowledge and experiences.

My Ku - Eyi. From my name Eyiowuawi

Next step is to explore your Ku's storage of memories.

## Memory Probe 

There are 2 exercises for this memory search both of which are best done in a sort of meditative state.

1. Treasure hunt
Invite your Ku to play with you. Name a memory that you wish to recall and dwell in it. Challenge your ku to immerse and remember as much as possible about the event. You may also ask it to explore it's own favourite memories. In this way you understand the underlying mechanisms or events and belief that make up your subconscious.

2. Trash collecting
With trash collecting, you tell your Ku to focus on your worst memories and try to identify patterns and complexes. These are usually the things blocking your development 

## Symbol Talk

 The ku can understand or communicate in symbols better than words. So you can prompt instead that it gives you a symbol to represent it's feelings on a particular topic that you provide it 

## Backtalk

You can prompt your Ku and explore it's feelings on a particular goal or venture by making statements towards them and exploring the responses and feelings that these statements birth. The statements out BBC-
I have the power (ability) to . . .
I have the right (I deserve) to . . . 
I have the desire to . . .
I have the will (determination) to . . .
