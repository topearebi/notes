# Digital social spaces

## Idea summary:

Social media identity

Personas and account incarnations

Trolling as an expression of a social skin/mask

The meta verse as an extension

The digital space has become another environment within which we can play social games. The digital environment also add the advantage of being mostly anonymous. This allows humans express social skinning in more extreme conditions

Whereas, previously every mask you wear is simply oa portrayal of yourself that you must master and still optimise towards doing good or gaining status. There is inherent risk

With digital skins, a lot of the risk is removed. You can create anonymous accounts with no repercussions to your irl status. 

Digital identity can be divorced. For the adept, it can serve as a training ground. For experimentation of the consequences of opinion.  

For the malintent, troll accounts have become common

Blockchain identity

Deanonymisation

## Idea context:

Stretching the idea of social skins into the digital environment
