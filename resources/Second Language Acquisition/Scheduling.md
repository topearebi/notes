## Part 1: The Chronological "Waterfall" (Months 1–3)

You do not start with a balanced week. You start by cascading the pillars into your routine as your cognitive bandwidth allows.

### Phase 1: The Acoustic Boot-Up (Weeks 1–2)
- **Focus:** 85% Pillar 1 (Phonetics) / 15% Pillar 2 (Structure - Typology only)
- **Action:** You are living in ABX Anki decks and mirrors. The only "Structure" you care about is reading your Language Typology audit so you know what you are getting into. No vocabulary is memorized. No output is generated.

### Phase 2: The Blueprint Assembly (Weeks 3–5)
- **Focus:** 20% Pillar 1 (Maintenance) / 60% Pillar 2 (Structure) / 20% Pillar 3 (Vocabulary - Tier 1 only)
- **Action:** Phonetics drops to a quick daily warm-up. You are now deploying the "Golden 13" diagnostic sentences and mapping the Syntax Highway. You introduce vocabulary, but only the Tier 1 "Glue" (conjunctions, prepositions) needed to hold the Golden 13 together.

### Phase 3: The Lexical Engine (Weeks 6–8)
- **Focus:** 10% Pillar 2 (Maintenance) / 70% Pillar 3 (Vocabulary) / 20% Pillar 4 (Output)
- **Action:** The structural rules are mapped. You now aggressively acquire Tier 2 (Action Chunks) and Tier 3 (Concrete Clusters). You begin Pillar 4's "Substitution Protocol" in a highly controlled, slow environment.

## Part 2: The "Steady State" Weekly Balance (Month 3+)

Once the foundational heavy lifting is done, the learner shifts from sequential phases into a balanced weekly routine. At this point, the framework operates as an integrated, self-sustaining loop.

Here is what that balanced, 7-day steady-state schedule looks like (assuming roughly 60 minutes of active study per day):

| Day | Primary Focus | The Operational Routine (60 Mins) |
|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | Input & Mining | 45m: Narrow Input (reading/listening to native media).<br>15m: Mine the text for new Tier 4 "Identity" vocabulary and log them into Anki. |
| **Tuesday** | The Engine (Vocab) | 20m: Anki SRS Reviews (Clearing the backlog).<br>40m: Building visual/cloze cards for the words mined on Monday. |
| **Wednesday** | Output & Syntax | 30m: Substitution Protocol. Swap Tuesday's new vocabulary into native syntax Skeletons.<br>30m: Write out 5-10 complex sentences to stress-test Pillar 2 rules. |
| **Thursday** | Input & Prosody | 45m: Narrow Input (Audio focus).<br>15m: Prosodic Shadowing (Pillar 1). Mumble along to internalize the target language's "Clock." |
| **Friday** | Output & Feedback | 45m: Active Output (Speaking/Writing).<br>15m: Submit 5% of this output to an AI/Tutor with a highly specific Diagnostic Request (Pillar 4). |
| **Saturday** | Script / Free Play | 30m: Dedicated orthography/script practice (if using Hanzi, Kanji, Arabic, etc.).<br>30m: Zero-pressure, extensive input (watch a movie, no pausing, no dictionary). |
| **Sunday** | System Rest | 15m: Anki SRS Reviews only (Do not let the algorithm pile up).<br>45m: Rest. Let the brain consolidate the week's neural pathways. |
