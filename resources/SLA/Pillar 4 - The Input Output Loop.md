## 4.1 The Input Strategy (Narrow Input)
**Order of Implementation:** 1. Select a highly specific, niche topic that interests you (e.g., "Making Pour-Over Coffee" or "Tech Startup News"). 
2. Gather 3 to 5 different native resources (articles, YouTube videos, podcasts) exclusively on this exact topic.
3. Consume them sequentially. The domain-specific vocabulary will naturally repeat, artificially inflating your comprehension rate and deepening neural pathways.

**Suggested Sources:** YouTube (search using target language keywords), Wikipedia (reading the same historical event in the target language), LingQ (for importing and tracking reading).

## 4.2 The Substitution Protocol (Active Production)
**Order of Implementation:** 1. Set a timer: 10 minutes of input (reading/listening). 
2. Stop and extract one structurally sound "Skeleton" sentence you just encountered.
3. Spend 2 minutes substituting the native nouns/verbs with the Tier 3 and Tier 4 vocabulary you built in Phase 3. 
4. Speak the new sentence out loud to physically prime the syntax.

**Suggested Sources:** Your own Phase 3 Anki deck, a notebook specifically dedicated to logging "Skeletons."

## 4.3 The Feedback Loop (Targeted Audits & Shadowing)
**Order of Implementation:** 1. During your input phase, perform "Prosodic Shadowing" (mumbling along to match rhythm) for at least 3 minutes.
2. At the end of your study session, take 2-3 sentences you created during the Substitution Protocol.
3. Submit these sentences to a native speaker or an AI language model with a strictly defined "Diagnostic Request" to prevent feedback noise.

**Suggested Sources:** ChatGPT/Claude (acting as a strict syntax checker), iTalki tutors, HelloTalk language partners.

### Template: The Targeted Diagnostic Prompts

Keeps your working memory focused solely on the physical mouth movement. |
