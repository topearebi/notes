# Self Image and Perception

> **Core Idea:** (One sentence summary)

---

## ✍️ Content
In many ways, our self image and the tone of it, whether that is positive or negative, is largely influenced by how we are experienced by other people. And even at that, how we believe we are experienced by other people. Our experience of their experience of us. A perception of a perception. Never mind that they do not understand us. Our intents and motivations. Our desires. Never mind that we do not understand their. Yet despite these filters and half untruths and untruths, we are driven by them. A perception of derision may lead us to anger or shame. Interest may lead to bravery. Love may lead to courage, or folly. 

Yet what is truly amazing is not the chaotic, haphazard nature of this system, but rather, that this system can in fact be quite expertly hijacked. There are people who, with specific, focused and dedicated study and experimentation, quite cleverly are able to hijack this flawed perception system in order to initiate triggers and induce perceptions that shape our actions. Despite our clear and obvious individuality across the 7 billion of our species, anyone from corporations armed with focus groups, feedback forms and user satisfaction surveys to individuals fortified by sales, personal development and para-psychology books, are able to quite literally manipulate into producing responses which are favourable to them, and we constantly, everyday, none the wiser.
