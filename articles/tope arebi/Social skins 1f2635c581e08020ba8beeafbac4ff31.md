# Social skins

## Idea summary:

An exploration of the true self Vs the social self and the idea of social masks. 

Segue into an analysis of authenticity as a social media strategy in this context of inauthentic social personas

## Idea context:

In protest of our reptilian overlord

Robert Greene’s interview with diary of a CEO on power and seduction in which he quotes the idea of the public self as a role to be played in social gatherings, and a mask to be shed once one arrives home. An idea that was quite common place pre social media and smartphones. 70z 80s?
