### Introduction/Hook

- What is the objective?
- Justifiication. Why are you writing about this topic?
- What is lacking in the current conceptualisation?s
    - Address the broader concern to society.
    - The specific concern to the individual

`Why are you writing about this? Why should they read it?`

### Context

- What is the history of this concept?
    - Explore its origins, causes or foundation.
    - Where does the problem come from? What problems or conditions necessitate it?
- Relevant examples and instances from history.

`Why does this exist? How did it come about? The concept as a cause Entertain the reader`

### Consequences of the concept

- Implications on the present. What are the conditions or institutions that have arisen from this concept.
- If they are bad, can they be remedied? If good, how can they be maintained?
- What is the cost of maintaining the status quo?
- What can we profit from changing our position?
- Do we need to change our position?

`Format: Argument statement. Concession. Elaboration. Conclusion`

### Antithesis. Counterargument

- Explore an alternative position
`Format: Position statement. Argument. Elaboration. Conclusion.`
