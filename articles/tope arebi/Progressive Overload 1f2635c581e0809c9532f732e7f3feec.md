# Progressive Overload

To get stronger at the gym, you need to  increase the demand or strain you place on your body over time. Even if you’re not aware of the concept of progressive overload, you naturally do this by lifting what you can at a certain point in time, and increasing it over time as your body adapts to the tasks you are giving it.

There are generally 4 ways to do this

- increase weight
- increase reps
- reduce rest
- vary leverage

What is common here is that you are doing more of the thing that you are trying to be better at.

The same thing  applies to pretty much every other area of life. The same ability that drove our adaptations as a species, will help you learn that new skill. 

Just get those reps in. And remember to rest adequately.
