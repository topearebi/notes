### Massage session tips

- Choose a vegetable oil, except peanut or corn oil, for the massage base. Consider coconut oil for its clean properties or safflower oil for affordability.

- Scent the oil with essential extracts, perfume, or lemon juice to create a desired mood.

### Creating a relaxing massage ambience:

- Lighting: Use soft, indirect lighting like a lamp or candles.
