> "three of anything is often enough to reliably recognise the beginning of a pattern"
> "If you want to physically take control of a submissive you can just say, 'Stand up!', but it'll be stronger if you give three orders, like, 'Stand up! Lift your head up straight! Shoulders back!'"
> "If you're using a cane or some other striking implement on the submissive the first stroke is going to impact them consciously, and the third and subsequent strokes are going to impact them primally"
>
> — [[The Control Book]]

This is an interesting one because I think "go" here is to be clear and also to overload primary resistance. A simple order might trigger a sort of instinctual kickback, but then repeating the order actually in different ways and in that form of three (potentially, I'm assuming that's the psychology here) could sort of avoid that initial shock and sort of force them into processing mode and compliance, which is a very interesting insight.
